Privacy policy (English)

In short: Dackelbrücke (Dachshund Bridge) does not collect any personal data.

  • The app needs no account and no sign-in.
  • The app itself never connects to the internet. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking services.
  • You buy the optional fan pack through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment or account data. The app only learns whether the fan pack was bought.
  • Only game progress is stored on your device: record, bones, unlocked items, daily goals, achievements, statistics and settings. This data never leaves your device, except through your own device backups (e.g. iCloud Backup by Apple).
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.
